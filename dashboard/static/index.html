What am I looking at? click to collapse

The Brain tab shows what the bot is THINKING right now — every ~10 seconds it refreshes.

  • v11 Engine card — overall health: how many strategies are loaded, last 5-min bar processed, current NY-time bucket, ATR (NQ's avg range), how many trades have fired today.
  • Live NQ-ES Divergence Z-scores — the bot's core signal. NQ and ES move together 99% of the time. The Z-score measures how UNUSUAL the gap is right now. Negative = NQ has fallen more than ES (LONG bias). Positive = NQ has rallied more than ES (SHORT bias). Each card is a different "lookback window" (15-bar = last 75 min, 120-bar = last 10 hours). A card glows green/red when a strategy is firing on that window.
  • Closest to Trigger — the top 15 strategies sorted by how close they are to firing. FIRED = trigger hit right now. NEAR = within 0.3 of firing. idle = far from triggering. "Distance" shows how far the current Z-score is from the strategy's threshold.
  • Recent Fires — the last 50 actual entries the bot has placed (rolling history).

The bot only takes ONE position at a time. When multiple strategies fire on the same bar, the highest-Sharpe one wins.
