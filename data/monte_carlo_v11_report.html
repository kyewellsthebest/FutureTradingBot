Interpretation

  • Pass rate: % of MC trials where the account survived 180 days without busting trail or DLL.
  • Avg profit (survivors): average $ gain among the accounts that survived.
  • EV per attempt: pass_rate × avg_profit – (1 − pass_rate) × $165.0 fee. Positive EV means the funded-account model is profitable in expectation.
  • Strategy: if EV is positive at multiple sizes, pick the one with highest EV (or highest absolute profit if your capital is unconstrained).

The user's intuition is correct: even if 60% of accounts bust, if 40% survive and make $20K each, the EV per attempt is still strongly positive — and you can scale by buying multiple accounts.
