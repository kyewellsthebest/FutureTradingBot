Strategy mechanics

Trigger: NQ-ES return divergence Z-score

  • Z-score = ((NQ_pct_change_N - ES_pct_change_N) - rolling_mean_200) / rolling_std_200
  • Z < -threshold → NQ underperformed → LONG NQ (mean reversion up)
  • Z > +threshold → NQ outperformed → SHORT NQ (mean reversion down)

Context filters: NY afternoon time slices (13:00-13:30, 13:30-14:00, 14:00-14:30, 14:30-15:00, 15:00-15:30, 15:30-16:00, 14:00-16:00 full pm)

Stop / target:

  • Stop: 1.0 to 1.5 ATR (typical NQ ATR ~28pts → 28-42 pt stop)
  • Target: 2.0 to 4.0 ATR (56-112 pt target)
  • Realized RR varies per trade due to TIME exits
  • Max hold: 45-90 minutes

Entry execution: at NEXT 5-min bar's open after trigger, with 2pt entry slippage. Stop exit adds 2pt adverse slippage. Commission $0.40/side.

Recommendations for live deployment

  1. Pick 1-3 best strategies not 30 — reduces correlation and execution conflicts
  2. Start at 5-10 MNQ until balance is well above locked trail (e.g. balance > trail + $3K)
  3. Take payouts at $51-52K balance to reset buffer (Lucid allows withdrawals after profit threshold)
  4. 100K account is more forgiving than 50K due to wider buffer and DLL
  5. Run in paper mode 30+ days before funding to verify live edge persists
