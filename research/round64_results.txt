ROUND 64 (CORRECTED) - QUOTES A/B ON BP-FINAL
==============================================
CORRECTION NOTICE: the first pass of this round (64a/64b) reported a
significant "thin book wins" effect. That result was INVALID - the
quote parquet files are stored newest-first, and the original lookup
assumed ascending timestamps, so every trade was tagged with a wrong
book state. Round 64c re-ran everything on sorted data with 185
correctly-matched trades (vs 131 mismatched before). This file is the
corrected record; distrust anything quoting the earlier numbers.

Question (user): "does implementing quotes make our win rate higher /
the strategy more accurate?"
Method: every BP-Final trade Jun 8 - Jul 8 tagged with the true book
state at its signal moment; tercile splits + 20,000-sample bootstrap.

CORRECTED VERDICT: on 185 trades, NO quote feature improves BP-Final
at statistical significance (bar: bootstrap > 0.975 or < 0.025):
  spread (wide better)        P = 0.968   closest, still short
  quote rate (quiet better)   P = 0.036   short from the other side
  top depth                   P = 0.619   the old "finding" - gone
  imbalance instant/fast/slow P = 0.30 - 0.92   noise, non-monotonic
Win rate stays in a 32-52% band across every bucket with no stable
pattern. Bid/ask size imbalance in particular - the "know the bids"
signal - is structurally empty for us: averaged over even 1.5s the
MNQ book almost never leaves 47-53% balance, and round 38 already
showed the ms-scale version is spent long before 65ms latency.

Two directional LEANS worth re-testing when more quote days exist
(both fit one story - fades entered during book stress do better):
wide spread at signal, low quote-rate at signal. Neither is
deployable evidence today.

DECISION: BP-Final stays untouched. The quotes-as-filter door on the
deployed strategy is closed pending a larger sample. The quotes-as-
PRIMARY-signal hunt (completely different strategy class) is round
65/66 - see round65_results.
