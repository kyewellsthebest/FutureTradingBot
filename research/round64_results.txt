ROUND 64 - QUOTES A/B ON BP-FINAL: MEASURED VERDICT
====================================================
Question (user): "if we implement quotes into our strategy, does that
make our win rate higher / the strategy more accurate?"
Method: every BP-Final trade in the real-quote window Jun 8 - Jul 8
(131 trades quoted of 208 generated; 23 fell on days without quote
files) tagged with the actual top-of-book state at its signal moment.
Same trades, split by book condition. Tercile splits + 20,000-sample
bootstrap on every feature. No theory.

1. BID/ASK IMBALANCE ("knowing the bids"): NO.
   Fast (~1.5s), slow (~15s), and instantaneous imbalance - aligned
   with the fade direction or against it - all noise-compatible
   (bootstrap 0.85-0.96, bar is 0.975). Win rate sits in the same
   40-50% band in every bucket, and the fast/slow splits aren't even
   monotonic (middle tercile best). Also structural: summed over even
   1.5s, MNQ book imbalance almost never leaves 0.47-0.53 - there is
   nearly nothing to condition on. Confirms round 38: at 65ms retail
   latency the imbalance signal is spent before we can touch it.

2. BOOK STRESS: THE ONE REAL SIGNAL - and it's inverted vs intuition.
   - TOP DEPTH (bootstrap P=0.019, SIGNIFICANT): thin-book trades
     avg +$44.85 (WR 50%) vs thick-book -$28.33 (WR 39%).
   - SPREAD (P=0.970, just under the bar, same story): wide-spread
     trades avg +$51.97 (WR 52.6%) vs tight-spread -$14.00.
   Physics: a wide, thin book at the signal moment = a genuine
   liquidity vacuum = the panic move we fade overshot = harder snap-
   back. A tight, deep book = the move is real two-sided business =
   less edge. The "safe-looking" book is the bad one.

3. HONEST CAVEATS (why this is a LEAD, not a deploy):
   - 131 trades, one 4.5-week window, ~8 hypotheses tested; one hit
     at p<0.025 is exactly what multiple comparisons can gift you.
   - A depth/spread gate keeps only ~1/3 of trades - frequency drops
     to ~20/wk, so even if real it reshapes the strategy materially.
   - Quote-rate feature unusable (bursty duplicate timestamps in the
     feed); depth cuts (<=4.3 / >8 contracts) are regime-dependent.

DECISION: do NOT gate the deployed bot on this yet. Path to verdict:
accumulate more evidence cheaply - Tradovate already streams live
bid/ask to the bot, so a logging-only patch can record spread+depth
at every FADESZ signal during the ongoing validation weeks, building
the confirmation dataset for free with zero trading-logic change.
Re-test the depth gate when the sample reaches ~400+ signals.

Bottom line for the user: quotes do NOT raise win rate via imbalance
(measured, not guessed). Book STRESS at the signal moment is the one
quote signal with real promise - it marks which fades have the most
snap-back - but on 131 trades it stays in the "watch and verify"
column, not in the live engine.
