ROUND 69 - QUOTES INSIDE THE STRATEGY (user's framing): VERDICT
================================================================
Arms tested on 14 clean post-roll overlap days (a hostile stretch -
baseline BP itself ran -$438/wk here, incl. the red validation wk):

  PANIC-EXIT (bail when book gets frantic mid-trade): WORSE in all
    3 variants (-446 to -854/wk). It cuts the winners: the fade's
    profit comes from holding through the last shake.
  STRESS-OFFSET (deeper limit when spread wide): NEUTRAL - spread
    is rarely stressed enough at signal time to move the needle.
  CALM-WAIT (signal fires normally; the limit order waits within
    its 180s window until 1s quote-rate < k x 10-min median):
    IMPROVES on baseline across the whole k grid 0.4-0.7
    (-96/+257/-15/-36 vs -438), keeping ~95% of trades. Best cells
    ~+$400-700/wk better than baseline in this window.

MECHANISM DECOMPOSITION (the important part):
  - Pure-timing control (wait for calm but keep the ORIGINAL price
    level): WORSE than baseline (-346 to -945). The gain is NOT
    from timing.
  - The gain comes from RE-ANCHORING: while waiting out the panic,
    price falls further, and the limit re-references lower ->
    effectively an ADAPTIVE deeper offset that deepens only while
    panic persists.
  - Fixed off=4.0 control nearly replicates it (-41/wk) in this
    window - but the 2.5-year tape already ruled that deep fixed
    offsets lose long-run (round 43: off 1.0 won). The adaptive
    version is exactly what deep-fixed is not: deep only when the
    book says the knife is still falling.

HONEST STATUS: calm-wait is the second real quote product (after
the quote-rate gate - same mechanism family: the book's panic gauge
timing our liquidity provision). It cannot face the 2.5-year
judgment (no historical quotes). 14 days, bumpy k-surface, hostile-
regime window: NOT deployable evidence. Same path as the gate:
SHADOW-LOG live (record 1s quote-rate at signal + through the entry
window at every FADESZ signal) -> one logging patch validates BOTH
the gate and calm-wait on real sessions with zero trading change.
