Sweep 3: the missing cell - hold 15 (the alpha horizon) with low
  thresholds a 0.8-1.2: two discoveries that close the question:
  a) FREQUENCY IS EXIT-BOUND, NOT SIGNAL-BOUND. Even at a=0.8

     one position at a time x 15-min holds = the strategy's
     metabolic rate. More trades REQUIRES shorter holds; holds
     3-12min destroy the edge (verified in all 864).
  b) SUB-THRESHOLD SIGNALS ARE NET-NEGATIVE FILLER. Lowering a from
     1.2 to 0.8 ADDS trades but REMOVES money ($252 -> $142/wk at
     flat-1): weak signals displace strong ones via the busy chain.

  The honest frequency ceiling on this instrument at retail costs is
  ~65 tr/wk flat / ~107 tr/wk laddered - set by the 15-minute alpha
  horizon, which is physics, not preference. 200+ tr/wk with real
  multi-year profit does not exist here. BP-Final IS the maximum-
  frequency honest strategy this market offers.
