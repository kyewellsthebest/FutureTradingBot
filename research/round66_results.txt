ROUND 65-66 - QUOTE-PRIMARY STRATEGY HUNT: VERDICT
===================================================
Mandate: completely different strategy with quotes as the PRIMARY
signal; latency upgraded as far as needed; every instrument.
Data: 22 MNQ quote days (Jun 8 - Jul 7), 13 with tick overlap after
the June->September contract roll ate Jun 16-26 tick coverage.
(Quotes exist ONLY for MNQ - other instruments need fetches.)

ROUND 65 - WHAT THE BOOK ACTUALLY KNOWS (decay map, 22 days,
250ms grid, ~2.2M observations, latency 0-500ms):
  - Microprice tilt / short imbalance DO predict direction - at 1-5
    second horizons, worth 0.07-0.15pt per event. Fees + spread cost
    ~1.25pt. Under water 5x even at 0ms latency.
  - VACUUM continuation (thin+wide book, go with move): +0.55pt at
    0ms decaying to +0.13pt at 500ms. Latency-sensitive AND under
    cost at every speed.
  - Quote-rate is a superb VOLATILITY forecaster (IC +0.34 out to
    15m) but knows nothing about direction.
  - Two latency-PROOF candidates survived to round 66:
    IMB60-FADE (-11pt drift against extreme 60s book imbalance by
    5m) and THIN-MOMO (+8.2pt with the move during sustained thin
    book), both flat from 0ms to 500ms reaction time.

ROUND 66 - FAITHFUL SIMS OF THE SURVIVORS (tick fills, $1.50/RT,
market and passive arms, 40+ configs each):
  - THIN-MOMO: DEAD. Positive cells entirely outlier-driven -
    clip the top 3 trades and every configuration goes negative.
  - IMB60-FADE: the trap that justified this whole process. Headline
    +$790-1,021/wk, 63-65% WR, 100% positive days, latency-proof...
    and the daily breakdown shows ALL of it in Jun 8-11, the roll
    transition week, when the September book was thin and lopsided
    (Jun 9 alone: +$1,341 of +$2,054). In the post-roll liquid book
    - today's actual market - |imb60| p90 collapses from >0.08 to
    ~0.02 and the strategy barely fires: +$36, +$3, +$31, -$30.
  - Recalibrated post-roll grid (th 0.02-0.06 x hold 2/5/15m, 7
    days): negative in 14 of 15 cells; the one positive cell is 3
    lucky trades (clip3 = -$1). IMB60-FADE is a ROLL-WEEK ARTIFACT.

THE LATENCY QUESTION, ANSWERED WITH NUMBERS: upgrading reaction
speed (200ms -> 65ms -> 0ms) unlocks nothing. The fast book signals
are worth fractions of the ~1.25pt round-trip cost even at zero
latency - they are for firms paying $0.10/side with colocation. The
slow book signals don't need speed. No infrastructure upgrade is
justified by measurement.

STATE OF THE HUNT: on available data, no quote-primary strategy
survives honest testing. This is NOT the final word - the sample is
13 overlap days, 4 of them roll-distorted. The verdict hardens or
breaks with: MNQU6 ticks Jun 8-26 (aligns ticks with the quotes we
already have), MNQ quotes Jul 8+ (extends the clean post-roll
window), MES/MGC quotes+ticks (only instruments whose friction
leaves any room). Quote-rate-as-vol-forecast is the one genuinely
new asset from this round - a possible future GATE for sizing/
pausing FADESZ, to be tested when the post-roll quote sample is big
enough to split.
