**THE SYNTHESIS.** Ledgers 21, 23, 24, 25 and 26 are five independent measurements of one fact: NQ's price path is a martingale at every scale tested. Average swing is 2.0x its threshold at every threshold (random-walk signature); 26 billion entry conditions match shuffled data; swing length will not separate; no exit target helps; no bracket beats its coin-flip rate. No stopping rule beats a martingale — that is a theorem, and it retroactively explains every null in this file.

What it does NOT cover: information outside the price path. Ledger #17 is the standing counter-example — book imbalance from L3 data was real and holdout-positive, because the resting order book is not in the chart. That, and cost reduction (bonds: $15.62-$31.25 per tick against the same $0.74 commission), are the only two directions left that this body of work has not already closed.

**CLOSING SUMMARY OF THE PRICE-PATH PROGRAMME.** Six independent measurements, each with matched controls, all agreeing: (a) average swing = 2.0x its reversal threshold at every scale from 2 to 80 points, the random-walk signature; (b) 26.1B tick-event configurations produce a 1.02x lift over a circular-shift null, and NQ specifically comes in at 0.909x, below chance; (c) swing length is unpredictable — the best of 13 causal features captures 3% of the available prize; (d) no exit target from 1 to 50 points beats waiting, at any swing size; (e) a +12/−6 bracket wins 33.28% where geometry gives 33.33%, and none of 56 bracket shapes in either direction deviates; (f) 103,680 structural strategies with variable geometry produce a best result WEAKER than the noise expectation, and that best result retains −19% out of sample. Nothing in the price path of NQ, at any scale tested, at any frequency tested, with any exit rule tested, survives a matched control. Three of these conclusions required catching errors in my own instruments first — an unsorted tape, a censoring bias, and a look-ahead in a feature — each found by a control rather than by inspection.
