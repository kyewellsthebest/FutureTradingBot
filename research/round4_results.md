## Round 5 proposal (FYI, since round 4 already PASSED)

Round 4 already passed with 17 strategies meeting ALL hard requirements.
Several questions remain for Round 5:

1. **Robustness on a held-out window** — every strategy in Round 4 was
   fit on the same 60-day window. Re-test the top 5 INV15s variants on
   different 60-day windows (offsets 5_000_000_000, 6_000_000_000,
   7_000_000_000) to confirm out-of-sample stability.

2. **Slippage stress** — sub-minute strategies fire signals on tiny
   2-3pt impulses with 2-4pt stops. If real-world fill slippage is 1pt
   instead of 0.5pt, expected P&L drops dramatically. Stress test:
   ENTRY_SLIP=1.0, STOP_SLIP=1.0, ENTRY_SLIP=1.5 — does the strategy
   still pass?

3. **Latency stress** — in live we may not see the bid/ask trigger
   instantly. Add 100ms / 500ms / 1s latency to the tick stream.

4. **Microstructure check** — INV15s_imp2_s4t12 fires 1,384 times/day.
   Roughly 1 trade every 25 seconds during market hours. Confirm we
   are not double-counting the same impulse with overlapping setups.

5. **Per-instrument validation** — the 60-day USTECH data may have
   regime characteristics that benefit this strategy (chop vs trend).
   Test on different instrument data if available.

6. **Live-bot wiring** — the 15-second pullback strategy needs the bot
   to subscribe to a 15-second bar builder and fire impulse/pullback
   logic at the correct cadence. Verify the live bot's bar boundary
   alignment matches the simulator's `int(ts // 15)` bucketing.

### Caveat — possible look-ahead in 15s strategies?

The Top 5 INV15s strategies fire 60-100K trades over 53 days. Win rate
is 56-67% with $/trade of $4-10. These numbers are *too good* to take
at face value without follow-up validation:

- 15-second bar pullback on 2pt impulse over 4 bars = trades when
  price moves ~2pt in 1 minute, fires pullback fade, exits within
  ~120s with 9-12pt target. Median target of 4-10x stop.

- Investigate: Does the bar builder maybe close a bar AND emit the
  setup AND let an entry trigger fire on the SAME tick that closed
  the bar? In sim_honest_battery this can't happen because the bar
  closes only when a *new minute* arrives — the same tick that
  builds the new bar can also trigger entries on stale pending
  setups.

- Round 5 must add an "embargo" check: the very first tick of a new
  bar cannot trigger entry on a setup that was just *created* by the
  bar that just closed. Otherwise entries fire at the exact close
  price of the bar, which is unrealistic.
