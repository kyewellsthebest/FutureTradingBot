ROUND 73 - DEALER-GAMMA (GEX) FROM 2Y RECONSTRUCTED OPTIONS: VERDICT
=====================================================================
Data: 2y QQQ options daily bars (132k contracts, 2.0M contract-days)
fetched via GitHub Actions. Build: underlying from put-call parity
(500 days, sane 475->695 path), 1.16M BS IV inversions, gamma per
contract-day. No historical OI exists anywhere at this tier, so two
proxies tested; BP-Final daily P&L re-simmed 2024-07 -> 2026-07
(417 overlap days) as the target.

PROXY 1 (daily-volume weighted): FAILS the mechanism check
(corr(lag-1 NGP, |move|) = +0.05, theory demands negative).
Its regime nulls are therefore uninformative. Discarded.

PROXY 2 (cumulative-volume synthetic OI): mechanism flips to the
correct sign (-0.072) but weak. Regime results, lag-1 causal:
  NGP > 0 (long-gamma days):  n=170  avg +$31.04/day  49% pos days
  NGP <= 0 (short-gamma):     n=247  avg -$32.82/day  41% pos days
  bootstrap P = 0.916  -> RIGHT DIRECTION, NOT SIGNIFICANT (0.975).
Flip-distance gradient from round 73a also points the same way
(price far above flip = bad for fades, 55%->30% pos day gradient).

HONEST CLOSE: the theory's fingerprints are visible in both the
mechanism and the regime split, but a volume-derived proxy cannot
prove it - reconstruction has hit its ceiling. The decisive
instrument is the TRUE-OI daily snapshot pile (building since
Jul 18, one command/day). At ~30 snapshots, plug real OI into this
round's prebuilt harness (bp_daily_pnl_2y.csv + gexmap builders)
for the clean verdict; at ~60, level strategies (flip/pin zones).
