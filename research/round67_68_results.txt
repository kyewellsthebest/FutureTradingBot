MECHANISM: quote-rate is the book's volatility forecast (IC +0.34
out to 15m). A frantic book at signal time means the panic move is
still being fueled - the fade gets run over. A calm book means the
move is exhausted - the snap-back works. This is the first quote
signal that (a) survives the corrected pipeline, (b) grows stronger
with sample size, (c) has a mechanism we independently measured.
